Terms & Policies

Terms & Conditions

By engaging Kaung Myat Kyaw for creative strategy services, the client agrees to a monthly retainer arrangement. Scope of work, deliverables, and reporting cadence are agreed in writing (email is acceptable) before work begins. Invoices are issued monthly in advance of each service period unless otherwise agreed.

Delivery Policy

Services are delivered on an ongoing monthly basis for the duration of the retainer. Deliverables (research summaries, ad copy, creative briefs, performance reviews) are provided digitally via email or a shared workspace as work is completed throughout each monthly cycle. There is no physical shipping involved, as all services are digital and consulting-based.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Either party may cancel the engagement with 14 days' written notice. If a client cancels partway through a paid month, fees for days not yet worked are refunded on a pro-rated basis. Fees already earned for work delivered are non-refundable. No refunds are issued for dissatisfaction with ad performance, as advertising results depend on factors outside the strategist's control (market conditions, budget, platform algorithms, product-market fit).
